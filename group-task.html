Part 3: Collaborative Writing Workshop

Co-constructing and peer-evaluating extended legal responses.

Your Group Challenge · VCE-Style Question (6 marks)

“Evaluate the effectiveness of the jury system in criminal trials.”

Use the four-step Evaluate formula and colour-code each part of your group response.

Workshop Workflow

Phase 1 · Before class

Draft Together

In your Padlet column, co-author a balanced Evaluate response using the colour code.

Phase 2 · In class · 20 mins

Peer Review

Use Two Stars and a Wish. Group 1 → 2, Group 2 → 3, Group 3 → 1.

Phase 3 · In class · 10 mins

Revise & Submit

Improve your draft from feedback, then get teacher sign-off.

1 Definition 2 Strengths 3 Weaknesses 4 Verdict
